Hold

Parents refresh leisure-centre booking pages for weeks to get a child into swimming lessons. Hold checks the live timetables of 510+ UK centres every few minutes and emails them the moment a space opens. Free, no account needed.

Its October report found 37% of 49,339 kids' swimming classes fully booked, and more than half in some cities.

How I build

Judgement in, software out.

  1. Start with a real question

    Every project began with something I noticed. Graduates can't afford CoStar. Parents can't get a swimming place. Fans love spotting talent before anyone else.

  2. Direct the build

    I write the spec, make the product calls and direct Claude to write the code. I had no coding background, so I learned to read, test and question everything it produces.

  3. Ship it and be honest about it

    Everything here is deployed with real data. The West End forecast is backtested against a simple baseline, and Hold publishes how it measures what it reports.
